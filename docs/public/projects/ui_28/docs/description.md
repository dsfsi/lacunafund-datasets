This initiative builds multimodal text and speech corpora for the Rapa Nui and Mapudungun languages (including its Pehuenche, Guluche, Lafkenche, and Huilliche variants) to reverse their exclusion from the digital world. Created in direct response to community needs, it expands on a previous machine translation prototype to include diverse dialects and crucial speech data for the many speakers who are not literate in their native tongue. The open-source corpora will allow local and international developers to create more robust and inclusive language technologies, such as accurate speech-to-text systems. This empowers indigenous communities in Chile to use their languages in daily digital life, safeguarding ancestral knowledge and fostering cultural preservation through technology.
