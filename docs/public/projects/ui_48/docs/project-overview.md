# Brain Tumor Segmentation Africa (BraTS-Africa) Dataset

## Overview
The BraTS-Africa dataset is an aggregation of magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scans from six centers in Nigeria aimed at providing a public dataset for the development of machine-learning solutions for the management of brain tumors in African patients. This dataset serves as a starting framework for future expansion in other regions of Africa.

## Dataset Information
- **Project ID**: 48
- **Domain**: Health
- **Data Type**: Medical Imaging Dataset
- **Model Type**: Image Segmentation
- **Dataset Size**: 584 images from 146 patient scans
- **License**: CC-BY 4.0 International

## Clinical Composition
### Patient Scans Breakdown
- **Total patient scans**: 146
- **Diffuse glioma cases**: 95 scans
- **Other CNS neoplasms**: 51 scans
- **Total processed images**: 584

### Expert Annotations
Expert radiologists provided detailed annotations for three distinct tumor sub-regions:
- **Enhancing Tumor (ET)**: Active tumor regions with contrast enhancement
- **Necrotic Tumor Core (NCR)**: Dead tissue within tumor mass
- **Peritumoral Edematous/Infiltrated Tissue (ED)**: Surrounding swelling and infiltration

## Research Significance
### Addressing Critical Gaps
Prior to this study, there was no known comprehensive annotated brain imaging dataset available to the public from Africa. This dataset addresses:
- Lack of African representation in medical AI datasets
- Need for population-specific neuroimaging data
- Requirement for locally-relevant machine learning solutions
- Clinical needs specific to Sub-Saharan African populations
