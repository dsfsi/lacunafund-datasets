# AI-Assisted Smartphone Microscopy for Detection of Diarrhea Parasites

## Overview
This dataset helps to detect diarrhea-causing parasites in resource-limited rural areas, particularly across the Global South, where access to expensive diagnostic tools is limited. It contains approximately 400,000 microscopic slide images from water, vegetable, and stool samples from four different provinces across Nepal, making it one of the largest datasets of its kind.

## Dataset Information
- **Project ID**: 49
- **Domain**: Health
- **Data Type**: Medical Imaging Dataset
- **Model Type**: Object Detection
- **Dataset Size**: Approximately 400,000 microscopic slide images
- **License**: CC-BY 4.0 International

## Sample Collection Protocol
### Water Sources (Multiple types sampled)
- Tap water
- Bottled water
- Lake water
- River water
- Pond water
- Stream water
- Spring water
- Wetland water
- Well water
- Borewell water

### Vegetable Samples
- Seven different types of vegetables analyzed
- Comprehensive coverage of common dietary sources

### Stool Samples
- Direct clinical samples for validation
- Cross-referenced with water and vegetable findings

## Target Parasites
The dataset focuses on detection of two primary diarrhea-causing parasites:
- **Giardia cysts**
- **Cryptosporidium cysts**

## Technical Implementation
### Image Capture Methods
- **Smartphone microscopy**: Accessible, low-cost imaging
- **Brightfield microscopy**: High-quality reference imaging
- **Dual-platform validation**: Ensuring diagnostic accuracy

### Data Platform Features
- Online data collection and annotation platform
- Multi-user upload capability with permission-based access
- Quality control review system
- Approval/rejection workflow for uploaded images
- Comment system for individual image feedback
- Date range and province-based filtering
- Comprehensive sample metadata tracking
