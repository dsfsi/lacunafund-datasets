Dataset: KenPos: Kenyan Languages Part of Speech Tagged dataset

Country/Region: Kenya

Description:
This project developed a Part of Speech (POS) Tagged dataset of 2 languages in Kenya: Dholuo and 3 Luhya dialects (Lumarachi, Lulogooli, and Lubukusi). The project tagged approximately 143,000 words.

Authors:
Florence Indede, Owen McOnyango, Lilian D.A. Wanzare (Maseno University), Barack Wanjawa, Lawrence Muchemi (University of Nairobi), Edward Ombui (Africa Nazarene University)
