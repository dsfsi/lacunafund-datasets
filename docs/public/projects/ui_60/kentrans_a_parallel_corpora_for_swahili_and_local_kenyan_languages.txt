Dataset: KenTrans: A Parallel Corpora for Swahili and local Kenyan Languages

Country/Region: Kenya

Description:
This project produced a parallel corpus between Swahili and two other Kenya Languages: Dholuo and 3 Luhya dialects (Lumarachi, Logooli and Lubukusu). A total of about 12,400 sentences were translated to Kiswahili.

Authors:
Lilian D.A Wanzare, Florence Indede, Owen McOnyango (Maseno University), Barack Wanjawa, Lawrence Muchemi (University of Nairobi), Edward Ombui (Africa Nazarene University)
