Dataset: IgboSynCorp: Dataset for Igbo Natural Language Processing Tasks

Country/Region: Nigeria

Description:
This dataset is the first spoken corpus of labelled and unlabeled datasets for Igbo Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks. It consists of approximately 40 hours of naturally occurring Igbo speech that is representative of all the dialects of Igbo.

Authors:
Gerald Nweya, Emeka Onwuegbuzia (Igbo NLP research collaboration)

Contact:
Gerald Nweya (GERALDNWEYA@GMAIL.COM) and Emeka Onwuegbuzia (EONWUEGBUZIA@GMAIL.COM)

This dataset is part of the Lacuna Fund initiative to support machine learning datasets for social good in the Global South.
